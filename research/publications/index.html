Reasoning Infrastructure

What's missing between capable systems and accountable ones — argued through the mechanism itself, then through two real, documented failures, then through the legal principle underneath all three.

  1. The Infrastructure of Reasoning — defines the missing infrastructural layer required to preserve reasoning continuity across human–AI systems
  2. The Red Signal Was Not Missed — a real-world failure showing how system signals can silently outrank human perception
  3. The Appearance of Successful Reasoning — an empirical and structural analysis of systems that appear to succeed while reasoning continuity is broken
  4. Why Intention Matters for Judgement — courts do not judge actions alone; they require intention, and modern systems don't preserve it

Interpretation, Drift & Cognitive Continuity

Research Papers

The Audited State Research Programme investigates Navigational Coherence: how understanding remains sufficiently coherent to support orientation, judgment, revision, learning, and action as systems, evidence, interpretations, and circumstances change.

The papers are independently assessable research contributions rather than a numbered series. All currently completed papers in the programme are now in circulation on SSRN and link here to their canonical records.
