🔴 Position Releases

This section highlights publications that define new risks, mechanisms, or structural responses requiring immediate attention.

A foundational question for any system design:

Is your system even legal?
Courts do not judge actions alone — they require intention.
Modern systems produce outcomes without preserving the intention that led to them.
If intention cannot be traced, responsibility cannot be assigned — a principle long established in law.
