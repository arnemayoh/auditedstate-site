Interpretation, Drift & Cognitive Continuity

  • Why Stable Understanding Requires Legible Ambiguity — Interpretive ambiguity as a foundation for continuity, governance, and distributed cognition
  • The Traffic of Reasoning — What traffic quietly teaches us about continuity, coordination, and shared understanding

  • Understanding Understanding — Why human understanding is fundamentally collective
