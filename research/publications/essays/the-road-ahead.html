We have spent years discussing the "threat" of AI, but we have ignored the infrastructure of our own progress. We do not make traffic safe by teaching every driver a private code of ethics; we make it safe by building lanes and requiring blinkers.

To move from the "Jungle of Systems" to the **Cognitive Super Highway**, we do not need "more" intelligence. We need more sensible structures.

1. A Shared Language (The Infrastructure)

Traffic only works because we all agree on what a red light means. In the realm of cognition, we are currently driving without lights. Meaning is reconstructed daily, and intent is lost to Semantic Drift.

The first requirement is the adoption of a Persistent Semantic Scaffold (PSS)—a shared lexicon and structure where terms like **Continuity**, **Lineage**, and **PIFR** are not just ideas, but the "lanes" that hold our thoughts in place.

2. Joining the Intention (The Signal)

In traffic, "responsibility is a lived experience." You signal your thought (a blinker), and then movement happens.

To make continuity real, we must commit to Structural Signaling. This means moving beyond the "Black Box" of Shadow AI and requiring every agent—human or synthetic—to package their reasoning into a Reasoning Vehicle.

Awareness is not a burden; it is the requirement for Flight. When you signal your intent, you aren't just following a rule—you are enabling others to continue where you leave off.

The Movement Begins

The road is already built in our bones. We know how to coordinate. We know how to share a path. We simply need to apply the "No Harm, No Foul" logic of the physical road to the digital mind. [cite: 2026-01-31]

When we stop hiding our reasoning and start signaling our intent, the **Great Accumulation** begins.
