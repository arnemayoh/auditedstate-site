Why Separate Entry Points?

A problem this broad is easier to enter through the questions you already ask. Each page begins in its field's own vocabulary, says plainly where this work meets it, and says what remains unproven. Across all of them one question recurs:

How can reasoning remain visible, accountable, and extendable across time as human–AI collaboration accelerates?

The architecture described across these pages exists as working infrastructure. What it needs is validation against real practice, with partners from these fields.

If your field is not here, tell us. The most useful thing a new field can do is say where the mapping holds and where it breaks.
