Architecture

The PKOS architecture describes how reasoning systems preserve continuity under accelerating human–AI collaboration.

Rather than focusing on outputs alone, the architecture connects reasoning, decisions, and institutional memory through a structured semantic infrastructure.

At its core, PKOS operates as a Persistent Semantic Scaffold (PSS) : a substrate that continuously preserves reasoning, decisions, and system state.

From Flow to Structure

The Concept Map describes the flow of reasoning. Architecture describes how that flow becomes durable.

The same reasoning loop is implemented structurally within PKOS.

Reasoning flows continuously. The scaffold ensures that each step remains preserved and reconstructable.

Human-in-the-loop (HuLoo) checkpoints occur both at promotion and during audit and repair, ensuring that responsibility remains visible whenever system state is created or revised.

Architectural Layers

The architecture distinguishes between layers of system operation:

  • Exploration Layer — human–AI reasoning through conjecture and analysis
  • Authority Membrane — the boundary where reasoning becomes accountable
  • Commitment Layer — durable decisions that alter shared system state
  • Semantic Layer (PSS) — persistent storage of reasoning, decisions, and lineage
  • Audit Layer — review, comparison, and repair of system state over time

These layers separate exploration from commitment while preserving continuity across both.

From Output to Lineage

AI systems expand the semantic surface area of institutions.

The critical question is not output volume, but whether reasoning remains connected to intention and justification.

Without continuity infrastructure, reconstruction cost rises until systems drift into interpretive entropy.

The Decision Path

When reasoning crosses the authority membrane, it becomes a durable decision.

  • Declared intention
  • Explicit justification
  • Structured validation
  • Execution
  • Recorded state

This path ensures that decisions remain reconstructable across time.

Inspectable Accumulation

Complex systems must be able to grow without losing interpretability.

PKOS achieves this through Inspectable Accumulation .

Each decision anchors a system state that can later be examined through traceable reasoning lineage.

Audit does not weaken the system. It strengthens it by preserving continuity through revision.

Architectural Outcome

The architectural goal is Cumulative Reasoning :

a system in which reasoning extends across time, rather than repeatedly reconstructing from fragments.

As AI accelerates knowledge production, this capability becomes essential.

Relationship to the System

PKOS does not exist in isolation.

It operates within a broader system of human intention, AI computation, institutional action, and ethical constraint — a cybernetic ecology.

Within this ecology, PKOS provides the structure that preserves reasoning continuity across domains.
