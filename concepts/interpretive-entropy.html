🔴 Interpretive Entropy

Interpretive Entropy is the gradual divergence between original semantic intent and current operational interpretation — the general condition under which reasoning coherence degrades, wherever it happens and whatever form it takes.

Use this when

Use this concept when you need the general name for degradation itself — not a specific symptom, but the underlying condition several specific symptoms are all expressions of.

Where you experience this

  • when a term's meaning has quietly shifted since it was first defined
  • when a decision made months ago no longer makes sense to anyone who wasn't there
  • when a system keeps producing outputs while understanding of it keeps getting harder to reconstruct
  • when nobody did anything wrong, and the drift still happened anyway

Opposing Force

Interpretive Entropy is the direct counterpart to Navigational Coherence — not one failure among several, but the general name for what happens whenever that sustained relation isn't actively maintained.

System Role

Within PKOS, Interpretive Entropy is the unifying condition underneath most of the site's other named failure modes. It is not a competing concept to Ephemeral Processing, Fragmentation, Semantic Drift, Reconstruction Cost, or Mutation Blast Radius — it is what all of them are measuring or describing, from different angles and at different scales.

The Physics of Decay

Consider a long-horizon human-AI collaboration engaged in evolving an institutional system over months or years. Definitions are refined, constraints are added, and governance structures are adjusted.

Interpretive entropy emerges from:

  • Bounded reconstructive cognition
  • Increasing cross-referential complexity
  • Mutation history accumulation
  • Defensive simplification under load

Not a Human Failure

Interpretive entropy reflects structural scaling pressure rather than individual failure. It is a natural condition of complex systems stretched across time, amplified by the speed of AI generation. Nobody has to make a mistake for it to happen — that is precisely why it needs infrastructure to counter it, rather than better intentions.

The Unifying Condition

Several of the site's other named pressures are not separate problems sitting alongside Interpretive Entropy. They are its specific mechanisms and measures:

  • Ephemeral Processing is where it starts — a single interaction's reasoning failing to persist
  • Sustained across many such interactions, it becomes Interpretive Entropy itself — the ongoing, compounding drift
  • Fragmentation is what it looks like once it has already won — the broken, disconnected state left behind
  • Semantic Drift and Semantic Surface Area describe specific dimensions of how it accumulates
  • Mutation Blast Radius and Reconstruction Cost are candidate ways of measuring how much of it has already happened

This mirrors how Navigational Coherence works on the other side: PIFR, Promotion Gate, and Requisite Variety aren't competing with Navigational Coherence, they're specific mechanisms serving it. Interpretive Entropy is the same kind of umbrella term, for the failure side.

Can It Actually Be Measured?

Not directly — the same argument Measurement as Convention makes about understanding generally applies here without exception. What the proxies above offer is a way of approximating it indirectly: Reconstruction Cost as a cost-based proxy, Mutation Blast Radius as a structural-risk proxy, Semantic Drift as a meaning-based proxy. None of them is Interpretive Entropy itself. Each is one candidate window onto it. This is an open research question, not a settled instrument — see the Research section's own open questions on this.

Formal Treatment

This concept is developed formally in the paper Interpretive Entropy: The Degradation of Reasoning Coherence in Fragmented Systems.

If Navigational Coherence is the name for what must be sustained, Interpretive Entropy is the name for what happens when it isn't — not one failure among several, but the general condition underneath all of them.
