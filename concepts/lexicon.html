A

Accountable Reasoning

Reasoning processes structured so that their assumptions, interpretations, and decisions remain visible, traceable, and correctable across time.

Assumption & Inference Review ↗

A reasoning practice that surfaces the assumptions and inference steps connecting premises to conclusions.

Audit & Repair ↗

The process through which a reasoning system recognizes consequences, acknowledges divergence, and continues with revised understanding.

Auditability

The ability to inspect, reconstruct, and evaluate how a system arrived at a given outcome.

In PKOS, auditability is achieved through traceable state and reasoning lineage, where each decision can be linked back to the reasoning artifacts that produced it.

This extends beyond output explanation to reconstruction of the reasoning process itself.

See also: Traceable State, Responsibility Boundary

Authority Membrane ↗

A structural boundary separating exploratory reasoning from authoritative mutation of state.

C

Collaborative Conjecture ↗

A phase in which human and AI reasoning interact to form new understanding, through iterative prompting, interpretation, and refinement.

Collaborative conjecture captures the moment where reasoning is actively shaped across human and system contributions, rather than produced by either in isolation.

In PKOS, this interaction results in structured reasoning artifacts (PIFRs), which preserve intention, assumptions, and intermediate understanding beyond the interaction itself.

See also: Hybrid Intelligence

Continuity ↗

The preservation of meaning, responsibility, and reasoning lineage across time.

Cumulative Reasoning ↗

The capacity of a reasoning system to extend prior reasoning across time.

Continuity Threshold ↗

The point at which systems can no longer maintain continuity of reasoning, causing understanding to shift from cumulative development to increasing dependence on reconstruction.

System Recovery Principle ↗

The principle describing how systems respond when continuity breaks: either by reconstructing reasoning at increasing cost or by restoring continuity through structures that preserve reasoning across time.

D

Decision Lineage

The preserved chain connecting intention, justification, and execution.

Decision Memory ↗

The preserved reasoning and context required to reconstruct the conditions under which a decision was made.

E

Externalized Cognition

The process of representing and manipulating thought outside the human mind, through tools, symbols, and systems.

The Persistent Semantic Scaffold (PSS) can be understood as a system for externalized cognition that preserves not only information, but the reasoning that connects it.

H

Hybrid Intelligence

A field of research focused on collaboration between humans and artificial intelligence systems, often emphasizing interaction, augmentation, and joint decision-making.

Within PKOS, hybrid intelligence corresponds to the phase of Collaborative Conjecture, where human and AI reasoning interact to produce new understanding.

While hybrid intelligence typically focuses on interaction, PKOS extends this by preserving the reasoning generated in such collaboration as traceable and reconstructable artifacts.

I

Interpretive Entropy ↗

Divergence between original semantic intent and interpretation.

Interpretive Collapse ↗

A condition where reasoning disappears and interpretation replaces understanding.

P

PIFR ↗

A structured reasoning artifact that carries reasoning forward.

PKOS ↗

A conceptual architecture for preserving reasoning continuity.

Promotion Gate ↗

The boundary where exploratory reasoning becomes durable and consequential.

Provenance

The origin and history of a piece of data, including how it was produced, transformed, and used.

In PKOS, provenance is extended from data lineage to reasoning lineage. Each state retains a trace of the reasoning (PIFR) that produced it, including intention and justification.

See also: Traceable State

R

Reasoning Continuity ↗

The preservation of reasoning trajectories across time.

Reasoning Fragmentation ↗

The dispersion of reasoning across disconnected contexts.

Reasoning Acceleration ↗

The increasing speed of reasoning production and use in AI systems.

Reasoning as Signal ↗

A view of reasoning as movement across contexts, where PIFRs function as signals carrying assumptions, intentions, and intermediate understanding between states.

Responsibility Boundary ↗

The moment where reasoning becomes consequential and accountable.

Responsibility Attribution ↗

The assignment of accountability to actors at decision points.

S

Semantic Anchor ↗

A concise statement that stabilizes the interpretation of a concept.

Semantic Drift ↗

Gradual shift of meaning across time and contexts.

Semantic Surface Area ↗

The total network of semantic dependencies in a system.

T

Traceable State ↗

A system state that retains a reference to the reasoning artifact (PIFR) from which it originated, enabling reconstruction of intention, justification, and prior context.

Trace Inheritance

The principle that every state inherits a minimal trace from its originating reasoning artifact, including intention, justification, and lineage reference.

Core Meta-Concept

Continuity Under Acceleration

Preserving meaning, responsibility, and reasoning continuity under AI acceleration.
