PKOS Lexicon

The lexicon defines the canonical vocabulary used throughout the PKOS framework. Each concept provides a stable reference point for discussions of reasoning infrastructure, governance architecture, and cumulative knowledge systems.

D

Decision Lineage

The preserved chain connecting intention, justification, validation, execution context, and recorded state of a decision.

Decision Memory ↗

The preserved reasoning and context required to reconstruct the conditions under which a decision was made.

I

Interpretive Collapse ↗

A condition where decisions remain visible, but the reasoning behind them disappears, leaving interpretation to replace understanding.

P

Promotion

The transition from exploratory reasoning to a durable decision that alters shared institutional understanding.

Promotion Gate ↗

The structural boundary where exploratory reasoning becomes consequential by entering durable, shared, or institutional state.

R

Reasoning Continuity ↗

The preservation of reasoning trajectories across time so that knowledge systems can extend prior understanding rather than reconstruct it.

Reasoning Fragmentation ↗

The dispersion of reasoning across disconnected contexts, making reasoning trajectories difficult to reconstruct or extend.

Reasoning Acceleration ↗

The increasing speed at which reasoning artifacts are generated, interpreted, and acted upon in AI-mediated systems.

Responsibility Boundary ↗

The moment where reasoning becomes consequential, when interpretation, selection, or promotion turns reasoning into responsibility.

Responsibility Attribution ↗

The assignment of accountability to specific actors at the point where reasoning becomes consequential.

S

Semantic Anchor ↗

A concise statement that stabilizes the interpretation of a concept across contexts.

Semantic Drift ↗

Gradual shift of meaning as concepts are reused and reinterpreted across time and contexts.
