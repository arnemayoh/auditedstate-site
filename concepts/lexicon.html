PKOS Lexicon

The lexicon defines the canonical vocabulary used throughout the PKOS framework. Each concept provides a stable reference point for discussions of reasoning infrastructure, governance architecture, and cumulative knowledge systems.

Definitions here serve as the semantic anchors for the site. Narrative pages may reference these concepts directly, allowing readers to move between explanations and the underlying vocabulary.

A

Accountable Reasoning

Reasoning processes structured so that their assumptions, interpretations, and decisions remain visible, traceable, and correctable across time.

Assumption & Inference Review ↗

A reasoning practice that surfaces the assumptions and inference steps connecting premises to conclusions so that reasoning structures can be examined and corrected.

Audit & Repair

A mechanism for reconstructing and evaluating prior reasoning states and correcting them without erasing the historical record of decisions.

Authority Membrane ↗

A structural boundary separating exploratory reasoning from authoritative mutation of institutional or canonical semantic state.

C

Collaborative Conjecture ↗

A human–AI reasoning practice in which hypotheses are explored, refined, challenged, and extended through iterative dialogue.

Continuity ↗

The preservation of meaning, responsibility, and reasoning lineage across time so that knowledge systems remain understandable and correctable.

Constraint as Enabler ↗

The idea that structural boundaries define direction and enable learning rather than merely restricting action; when transparent, constraint functions as governance.

Continuity Infrastructure

Architectural mechanisms that stabilize reasoning artifacts across time, preventing fragmentation and enabling cumulative learning.

Cumulative Reasoning ↗

The capacity of a reasoning system to extend prior reasoning across time rather than repeatedly reconstructing it.

D

Decision Lineage

The preserved chain connecting intention, justification, validation, execution context, and recorded state of a decision.

E

Epistemic Maturity ↗

The structured evolution of ideas through stages such as perspective, hypothesis, proposal, and institutional commitment.

Exploration

A provisional reasoning domain where ideas, interpretations, and hypotheses can evolve without immediate institutional consequences.

G

Governance vs Control ↗

The distinction between control, which restricts action directly, and governance, which preserves accountability and visibility within action.

H

HuLoo (Human-in-the-Loop Checkpoints)

Moments where humans interpret, validate, and assume responsibility for reasoning artifacts before they influence durable institutional state.

Human Agency ↗

The capacity of humans to initiate decisions, interpret reasoning artifacts, and accept responsibility for outcomes within human–AI collaboration.

I

Inspectable Accumulation ↗

The ability of a system to grow semantic complexity while preserving reconstructable reasoning lineage.

Inspectable Reasoning ↗

Reasoning processes that remain visible and reconstructable so that later observers can understand how conclusions were reached.

Institutional Learning

The capacity of institutions to refine decisions and knowledge through preserved reasoning records and feedback across time.

Interpretive Entropy ↗

Divergence between original semantic intent and operational interpretation in evolving institutional systems.

L

Labs

Experimental environments within PKOS where exploratory reasoning, hypotheses, and interdisciplinary ideas can develop freely.

M

Mutation Blast Radius (MBR) ↗

The scope across which a semantic or structural change propagates through dependent concepts or rules in a system.

P

PIFR (Pay-It-Forward Record)

A structured reasoning artifact that captures intent, assumptions, reasoning steps, constraints, conclusions, and criteria for future continuation.

PKOS (Personal / Professional / Pedagogical / Public Knowledge OS)

A conceptual operating system for accountable reasoning under AI acceleration.

Persistent Semantic Scaffold (PSS) ↗

A structured semantic substrate that anchors concepts and preserves reasoning continuity across evolving systems.

Promotion

The transition from exploratory reasoning to a durable decision that alters shared institutional understanding.

R

Reasoning Artifact

A structured record capturing the state and trajectory of a reasoning process so it can be examined and extended later.

Reasoning Infrastructure

Architectural mechanisms designed to preserve reasoning continuity, accountability, and traceability in human–AI collaboration systems.

Reasoning Traceability

The ability to reconstruct how a conclusion or decision was reached by preserving the trajectory of reasoning across time.

Reasoning Trajectory

The evolving sequence of reasoning steps that develop and refine an idea across time.

Reconstruction Cost ↗

The cognitive and procedural effort required to reconstruct reasoning lineage behind a decision.

S

Semantic Drift ↗

Gradual shift of meaning as concepts are reused and reinterpreted across time and contexts.

Semantic Surface Area (SSA) ↗

The total network of definitions, governance constraints, and semantic dependencies in an institutional system.

Shadow AI ↗

The use of AI systems without durable records of intention, reasoning lineage, or responsibility.

State Anchoring

A recorded snapshot capturing the reasoning context and semantic state at the moment a decision becomes durable.

T

Traceability

The ability to follow the reasoning lineage of decisions through preserved artifacts and structural records.

Core Meta-Concept

Continuity Under Acceleration

The central PKOS thesis: preserving meaning, responsibility, and reasoning continuity in systems operating under AI-accelerated knowledge production.
