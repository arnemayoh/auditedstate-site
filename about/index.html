About

Audited State exists to preserve human agency
by preserving continuity of understanding under acceleration.

Audited State has widened as the underlying question has widened. What began as a practical problem of preserving reasoning continuity in human–AI collaboration repeatedly appeared in other forms: as reconstruction cost in organizations, lost context in governance, discontinuity in learning, drift across systems, and the difficulty of preserving understanding as people, technologies, and institutions change. The work now spans public synthesis, research, continuity infrastructure, prototypes, and empirical investigation — not because these fields are being collapsed into one theory, but because the same suspected continuity problem keeps appearing between them. The diagram below is an orientation map of that programme: different vocabularies and different entry points, connected by one question — how can understanding remain coherent, recoverable, and capable of being carried forward?

The Programme

The work is deliberately distributed across several forms. The book The Wealth of Understanding develops the general-reader synthesis. The research programme separates the larger concern into narrower, contestable propositions. Infrastructure work explores how continuity might be supported in practice. Prototypes place those ideas into real use, and empirical studies ask what survives outside the theory. Each part is intended to inform, challenge, and revise the others.

Why “Audited State”?

The name refers not to control, but to the legibility of reasoning processes: whether later participants can reconstruct what was understood, why a decision made sense at the time, what remained uncertain, and where responsibility sits.
